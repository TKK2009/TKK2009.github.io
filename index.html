The Sugar Landscape

Analysis
1,143desserts

I collected the published nutrition data for five national dessert chains to find out what actually drives how much sugar someone eats. It turns out the chains are nearly identical once you account for portion size, and the size you order matters far more than where you order it. Two of the five chains do not publish added sugar at all.
